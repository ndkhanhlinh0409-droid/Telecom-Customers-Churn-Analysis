# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## Overview
This project analyses customer churn behaviour for a telecommunications 
provider to identify the highest-risk customer segments, quantify the 
financial impact, and deliver evidence-based retention recommendations. 
The analysis follows a structured end-to-end pipeline covering data 
cleaning, exploratory analysis, interactive visualisation and predictive 
modelling.

## Research Question
Which customer segments are most likely to churn, what is the financial 
impact, and what retention strategies can be recommended to reduce churn rate?

## Tools Used
- **SQL Server** — data cleaning, feature engineering and exploratory analysis
- **Power BI** — interactive dashboard for segment and revenue visualisation
- **R** — logistic regression for statistical validation of churn predictors

## Dataset
Public telecom customer dataset comprising 7,043 records and 21 variables, 
covering customer demographics, service subscriptions, contract details, 
billing information and churn status.  
Source: IBM Sample Dataset (Kaggle)

## Key Findings
- Churn rate stands at 26.54%, notably above the industry benchmark
- Month-to-month customers in their first year are the highest-risk segment
- Contract type and tenure are the strongest churn predictors across both 
  exploratory and regression analysis
- Fiber optic customers without tech support churn disproportionately 
  despite being the highest-value service tier
